# M3 Acquisition and Licensing Record

This record satisfies the M3 [data acquisition checkpoint](../plan.md#data-acquisition-checkpoint) for the quartz mineral record and the trigonal symmetry-operation registry. It covers the crystallographic data, habit form definitions, provenance representation, and the trigonal point-group registry subset established during M3.

The Materials Project is a publicly accessible database that does not require an account, click-through license, or paid access. Its data is licensed under CC-BY 4.0, which permits redistribution with attribution. The unit-cell parameters are well-established values for α-quartz, consistent across multiple crystallographic databases and literature sources. The two enantiomorphic space groups (P3_121 and P3_221) are sourced from separate Materials Project entries (mp-7000 and mp-6930 respectively).

| Integrity checks | Verify the source tag commit hash before regenerating; hash the generated artifact with SHA-256 and record it. Validate identity, closure, inverses, and metric compatibility in core tests. |

The trigonal registry uses the same pinned spglib v2.7.0 source as the M1 cubic registry. Hall 441 corresponds to space group 152 (P3_121, right-handed quartz). Its 6 general positions decode to 6 unique linear matrices forming point group 32. The generator script verifies the source hash, decodes the rotation matrices, deduplicates them, verifies group closure and inverses, and records the artifact SHA-256.

## Habit form definitions

The four shipped habits (Prismatic, Tessin, Cumberland, Pseudocubic) are defined using standard trigonal crystallographic forms in Miller-Bravais notation. The form indices ({10-10} prism m, {10-11} rhombohedron r, {01-11} rhombohedron z, {20-21} steep rhombohedron M, {02-21} steep rhombohedron Ψ) are established crystallographic notation, not acquired data. The Tessin habit uses steep rhombohedra (M, Ψ) to produce the characteristic tapered spindle shape; the other habits use the shallow rhombohedra (r, z). The symmetry-equivalent faces are generated by the core engine from the 32 point-group registry entry.

Habit development values are curated visualization parameters chosen to produce recognizable quartz habits, not measured quantities. They are identified as `curated` in the provenance record and distinguished from the `reported` crystallographic data.

### Asymmetry decision

The M3 asymmetry decision is resolved: the four shipped V1 quartz habits do not require within-form asymmetry. They use only form-level development controls (different development values for different forms), which are already supported by the existing morphology engine. Within-form asymmetry (unequal development of symmetry-equivalent faces) is explicitly deferred. This decision is recorded in the habit preset documentation and will be reassessed when selecting remaining habits in M5.

## Quartz handedness variants

The left-handed and right-handed quartz variants share the same point group (32) and unit cell but differ in their space group (P3_221 vs P3_121). Both variants are defined using sourced crystallographic data from the Materials Project (mp-6930 and mp-7000 respectively). The point-group operations for morphology are identical for both variants; the structural difference (screw-axis sense) affects atomic positions, not the morphology forms used in M3. The variants are represented as `MineralVariant` entries in the quartz record, each carrying its own crystallographic identity and source reference.

## Provenance representation

M3 extends the M2 provenance representation for the quartz record, following the [provenance contract](../data-model.md#scientific-confidence--provenance). The quartz record demonstrates:

* **Reported** crystallography: unit-cell parameters, crystal system, point group, and space groups covered by Materials Project references for both enantiomorphs.
* **Curated** morphology: habit development values identified as visualization parameters with a derivation statement.
