# M2 Acquisition and Licensing Record

This record satisfies the M2 [data acquisition checkpoint](../plan.md#data-acquisition-checkpoint) for the fluorite mineral record. It covers the crystallographic data, habit form definitions, and provenance representation established during M2.

The Materials Project is a publicly accessible database that does not require an account, click-through license, or paid access. Its data is licensed under CC-BY 4.0, which permits redistribution with attribution. The unit-cell parameter is a well-established value for fluorite, consistent across multiple crystallographic databases and literature sources.

## Habit form definitions

The three shipped habits (cube, octahedron, cubo-octahedron) and the available dodecahedron {110} form are defined using standard cubic crystallographic forms. The form Miller indices ({100}, {111}, {110}) are established crystallographic notation, not acquired data. The symmetry-equivalent faces are generated by the core engine from the m-3m point-group registry entry.

Habit development values are curated visualization parameters chosen to produce recognizable fluorite habits, not measured quantities. They are identified as `curated` in the provenance record and distinguished from the `reported` crystallographic data.

## Provenance representation

M2 establishes the minimum provenance representation for shipped mineral records, following the [provenance contract](../data-model.md#scientific-confidence--provenance). Each provenance entry identifies coverage, origin (reference or curated), status, and derivation for derived values. The fluorite record demonstrates:

* **Reported** crystallography: unit-cell parameters, crystal system, point group, and space group covered by the Materials Project reference.
* **Curated** morphology: habit development values identified as visualization parameters with a derivation statement.

This representation is applied to subsequent shipped records throughout M3–M5.
