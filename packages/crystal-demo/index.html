  • Reviewed Surface Profiles (SR5) — Quartz, calcite, and pyrite growth-face profile review with effective-profile status
  • Surface Rendering Overview (SR7) — All nine minerals with stable surface-detail controls, off/enabled comparison, and effective profile information
